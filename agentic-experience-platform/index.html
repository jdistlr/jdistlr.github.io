05 · Success without reset

When the tiny app becomes important, don’t start over.

Success can make software deeper or broader. Those are different moves.

Maturity

More assurance

Explore Share Operate Validate Enterprise

More assurance when policy, risk or business context requires it.

Evidence continuity

Source history, ownership, deployments, dependencies, access configuration and automated tests become inputs to formal assurance instead of being recreated manually.

Experience scope

More composition

Isolated Shared Composed Enterprise experience

More coherence when user journeys cross app boundaries.

Important distinction

A developer portal and a composed business experience solve different problems, even if they share some technical primitives.
